# Ethical use

Contract changes are not evidence of wrongdoing. Amendments can reflect legitimate changes in
scope, delivery timing, pricing, options, reporting, or data correction.

Users should:

- inspect the linked primary records
- seek surrounding tender, contract, audit, and agency context
- distinguish reported values from realised expenditure
- give organisations and suppliers a fair opportunity to explain changes
- avoid harassment, unsupported allegations, and automated profiling
- publish calculations and uncertainty alongside any conclusion

ContractDelta deliberately excludes unnecessary personal and contact information. Please do
not reintroduce it in issues, fixtures, exports, or derivative work.

Public availability is not a reason to aggregate personal information beyond the procurement
purpose. Supplier and agency names are retained for contract analysis; contact names, phone
numbers, email addresses, ABNs, and street or postal addresses are discarded.
